## 🔴 Ab aapko ye karna hai — priority order

### Week 1: Google Business Profile (sabse zaroori)

Ye 32% hai. Website se **do guna** important.

**1. Profile banao** — google.com/business

**2. Primary category** — ye single sabse powerful factor hai:
```
Primary:    Water Purification Company
Secondary:  Water Filter Supplier
            Repair Service
            Water Testing Service
```

**3. Service Area Business set karo** (address mat dikhao — aap doorstep service dete ho)
- Patna + 25 km radius

**4. Ye sections zaroor bharo** (log yahi khaali chhod dete hain):
- **Services** — har service ka 2-3 line description
- **Q&A** — khud 5-6 sawaal daal ke jawab do
- **Photos** — asli kaam ki, AI nahi

**5. Business name mein keyword mat thoosna** — `AquaNexa RO Service Patna Best Kent
Aquaguard` type naam suspension risk hai (score 125). Sirf `AquaNexa` ya
`AquaNexa RO Service` rakho.

**6. Hafte mein 1 post** — actual repair photos ke saath

---

### Week 1–ongoing: Reviews (20%)

**Target: hafte mein 2–3 reviews.** Total count se zyada **velocity** matters.

Har complete job ke baad WhatsApp pe ye bhejo:

```
Namaste [Naam] ji, aapka RO theek ho gaya —
umeed hai paani ab sahi aa raha hai.

Agar service achhi lagi to 30 second nikaal ke
Google pe review de dijiye, bahut madad hogi 🙏

[GBP review link]

— AquaNexa, 8969821440
```

**⚠️ Ye galtiyan mat karna:**
- Fake reviews mat kharidna (suspension score 132)
- Ek saath 20 reviews mat lena — "too fast" bhi flag hai (score 104)
- Har review ka reply do (no-response bhi factor hai)

**Milestone:**
| Reviews | Kya hoga |
|---|---|
| 15–20 | Credible dikhne lagoge |
| 50+ | Top-3 Local Pack ke liye ready |
| 100+ | 3.2x behtar ranking |

## Ek honest baat

Website ka kaam **poora ho gaya** — 15% factor pe aap 100% ho.

Par agar aap GBP nahi banate aur reviews nahi lete, to **website akeli
top pe nahi la sakti**. 52% weight wahan hai.

5,117 reviews wala competitor 5 saal se bana raha hai. Aap 6 mahine mein
usse nahi jeetoge. Par **teesre number pe aa sakte ho** — aur Local Pack
mein teesra number bhi kaafi kaam deta hai.
