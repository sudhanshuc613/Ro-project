# 🎯 Ab wo cheezein jo MUFT hain aur tera competitor nahi kar raha

## 1. 🔴 GBP Primary Category — #1 factor, 5 minute ka kaam, ₹0

> *"Whitespark's 2026 report says your **primary category is the single most important Google Maps ranking factor** for the local pack. **Not reviews. Not links.** Your category selection."*

**Aaj hi check kar:**
`Google Business Profile → Edit profile → Business category`

| Category | Sahi? |
|---|---|
| `Water purification company` | ❌ bahut general |
| `Water filter supplier` | ❌ tu supplier nahi, service wala hai |
| **`Water purification equipment supplier`** | 🟡 theek |
| **`Water treatment supplier`** | 🟡 theek |
| **`Repair service`** ya jo bhi RO repair ke sabse kareeb ho | ✅ specific |

**Rule: sabse SPECIFIC category chunno.** Aur **9 secondary categories** bhi bhar — wo free relevance hai. Zyadatar log 1 category daal ke chhod dete hain.

## 2. 🔴 "Open now" — 5th biggest factor, ₹0

> *"Whether a business is marked open at the moment someone searches is the **5th most important individual factor** in Map Pack visibility."*

Tere site pe likha hai **8 AM – 9 PM**. GBP pe bhi wahi hona chahiye.

**Sochne wali baat:** RO kharab hoti hai to log **subah 7 baje** ya **raat 10 baje** search karte hain (jab paani chahiye). Us waqt agar tu "Closed" dikha raha hai — tu us search se **gayab** hai.

**Karo:** GBP pe hours **7 AM – 10 PM** kar do (agar tu itni der phone uthata hai). Ya "Open 24 hours" agar emergency call leta hai.

Ye ek setting badalne se tu subah-shaam ki har search me wapas aa jayega. **₹0.**

## 3. 🔴 Reviews — tere dhandhe me 36% weight, ₹0

Tere paas **44 reviews** hain. Ye acha hai. Par:

> *"Businesses in the top 3 positions average **47% more reviews** than those in positions 4-10."*
> *"Recency: last week reads very differently from six months ago."*
> *"Velocity: a steady drip beats a one-time flood."*
> *"Response rate: are you replying, or just collecting?"*

### Ye teen sawal apne aap se pooch:
1. **Aakhri review kab aaya tha?** 6 mahine pehle? To Google samajh raha hai dhandha thanda hai.
2. **Kitne reviews ka tune reply diya hai?** Reply na dena = signal ki tu active nahi hai.
3. **Mahine me kitne naye aate hain?**

### Ek Reddit small business owner ne likha:
> *"We stopped offering discounts for reviews and started **texting a Google review link 30 minutes after the service call**. Our review count **tripled in four months** and our Maps ranking went **from page 2 to the 3-Pack**."*

**₹0 kharch. Sirf ek aadat.**

### Tera system — aaj se
```
Kaam khatam → paisa liya → ghar se nikalte waqt
→ WhatsApp pe review link bhej do
→ "Bhaiya/Didi, kaam pasand aaya ho to 1 minute me
   Google pe review kar dijiye. Bahut madad ho jayegi."
```

Roz 3 kaam = mahine me 90 mauke. 20% ne bhi kiya to **18 review/month**.

4 mahine me tere **44 → 100+** reviews. Aur wo bhi **fresh** — jo purane reviews se zyada weight rakhte hain.

**Aur har review ka reply do. 24 ghante ke andar.** Ek line kaafi hai: *"Dhanyavaad Rakesh ji, kabhi bhi zaroorat ho to call kariye."*

> ⚠️ **Kabhi mat karna:** review ke badle discount dena, ya customer ko bolna ki *"mera naam likhna"* — April 2026 se ye **Rating Manipulation violation** hai. Ban ho sakta hai.

## 4. 🔴 Photos — 20+ photos = 18% zyada clicks, ₹0

Google ka apna data:
```
Photos wale profile      → 30-50% zyada views

                         → 35% zyada website clicks
20+ photos wala          → 18% zyada clicks (5 se kam wale se)
```

**Tere paas phone hai. Har kaam pe 2 photo kheench:**

**Hafte me 6 photo × 4 hafta = 24 photo/month. ₹0.**

> ⚠️ Ek problem: `public/service/*.jpg` me jo photos hain wo **AI se bani hui** hain. GBP reverse image search se pakad sakta hai. **Asli photo daal**, apne phone se.
