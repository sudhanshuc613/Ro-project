# 5 Features Jo Aapko MUST Chahiye

Ye maine competitors ki weakness dekh ke chune hain — na ki "sabke paas hai isliye".

## 1. AMC Subscription Engine (Livpure model, local twist) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Kyun:** Livpure ne subscription se 14.5% YoY growth banayi. Patna mein **koi bhi** competitor auto-renewal AMC nahi chala raha.

**Business impact:** Ek repair = ₹500 one-time. Ek AMC = ₹2,799/year **recurring**. 100 AMC = ₹2.8 lakh guaranteed revenue.

**Kya banega:**
- Customer AMC kharide, DB mein subscription banti hai
- Har visit ka schedule auto-calculate
- 7 din pehle WhatsApp reminder
- Renewal se 30 din pehle auto-reminder
- Admin ko dikhe: is mahine kitne due hain

> Ye aapka #1 revenue lever hai. Isse pehle kuch mat banao.

## 2. Water TDS Map of Patna ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Kyun:** Koi competitor ye nahi kar raha. Ye pure SEO + trust weapon hai.

Har visit pe aap TDS naapte ho. Wo data collect karo → area-wise public map banao.

**SEO impact:** "Kankarbagh water TDS", "Patna water quality" — ye searches kisi ke paas nahi hain. Aap monopoly bana sakte ho. Backlinks bhi milenge (news, blogs).

**Trust impact:** "Aapke area ka TDS 850 hai, isliye 100 GPD membrane chahiye" — ye salesman nahi, expert ki baat lagti hai.

**Kya banega:**
- Technician har visit pe TDS entry kare
- Public page: `/patna-water-quality` — area-wise TDS
- Har area page pe live average dikhe

## 3. Service History + Machine Profile ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Kyun:** Eureka Forbes yahi se jeetta hai — customer lock-in.

Customer ka machine profile save karo: brand, model, kab kya badla.

**Business impact:**
- Aap jaante ho membrane 18 mahine purana hai → proactive call
- Customer doosri jagah nahi jayega (uska record aapke paas hai)
- Repeat revenue

## 4. Technician Live Tracking (Ola/Uber model) ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Kyun:** Patna mein customer ka #1 complaint — "technician bola aa raha hai, 4 ghante ho gaye".

**Kya banega:**
- Booking pe ticket link SMS/WhatsApp
- Customer live status dekhe: Confirmed → Technician assigned → On the way → Reached
- Technician ka naam + photo + phone

Ye chhota feature hai par **word-of-mouth** bahut banata hai.

## 5. Emergency Same-Day Slot (paid premium) ⭐⭐⭐

**Kyun:** Koi bhi Patna competitor guaranteed timing nahi deta.

**Kya:** ₹200 normal visit, **₹299 mein 2-ghante guarantee**. Late hue to visit free.

Log urgency ke liye paisa dete hain. Aur ye aapka average ticket badhata hai.
