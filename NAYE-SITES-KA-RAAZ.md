## 1. Tera URL path already EMD hai

```
rokadoctor.in/service-patna                → "service patna"
rokadoctor.in/service-patna/kankarbagh     → "service patna kankarbagh"
rokadoctor.in/service-patna/buddha-colony  → "service patna buddha colony"
```

→ Zyada asar, par duplicate content ka risk. **Main ye recommend nahi karta.**
