### 🔴 Ye galtiyan mat karna

| ❌ Mat karna | Kyun |
|---|---|
| Review ke badle discount dena | Google ki policy ke against — sab review udd jaayenge |
| Khud ke phone se review likhna | Same IP/device se pakda jaata hai |
| Rishtedaron se likhwana jo customer nahi hain | Fake pattern |
| Review kharidna | Permanent suspension |

### Review mein keyword aana chahiye

Customer ko halka sa direction de:

> "Agar likhein to bata dijiyega ki kaunse area mein hain aur kaunsi machine thi — usse doosre logon ko samajh aata hai."

Isse review aisa banega: *"Kankarbagh mein mera Kent RO theek kiya"* — ye **"kent ro service kankarbagh"** search ke liye ranking deta hai.

### Har review ka jawab dena — 48 ghante ke andar

Response rate bhi ranking factor hai.

Achhe review pe:
> "Dhanyawaad Rakesh ji! Kankarbagh mein aapke Kent RO ki service kar ke achha laga. Kabhi bhi zaroorat ho to 8969821440. — AquaNexa"

Bure review pe (kabhi ladna nahi):
