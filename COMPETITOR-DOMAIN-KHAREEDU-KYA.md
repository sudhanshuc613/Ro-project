# Sach #2 — Kharid bhi lo to kaam nahi karega

Maan le tune paisa de ke domain le liya. Ab kya hoga?

## Google ka official rule (2023 se)

> *"If a domain expires, and somebody buys that domain, **any signals the site had accumulated will not be transferred to the new domain owner**. Google knows when a domain expires."*
> — **Gary Illyes, Google Search Analyst**

Google ne iske liye ek policy hi bana di hai: **"Expired Domain Abuse"**.

## Aur asli logon ka experience

> *"We purchased two expired domains with solid backlink profiles and redirected them. Slight bump at first, but **within 2-3 months the site was hit with a manual action penalty**. We had to do considerable cleanup."*
> — Dave Kerr, Merged Dental Marketing

> *"I've tried forwarding links from old domains to new domains, and despite solid domain authority, **the results were quite lackluster**. I wouldn't recommend it for any whitehat SEO projects."*

**Manual action penalty** ka matlab: Google tere site ko haath se punish karta hai. Ranking gir jaati hai, wapas aane me mahine lagte hain.

# ✅ To kya karna chahiye — 3 asli option

## Option 1 — 🔴 Kuch mat karo, bas ye 3 kaam karo (₹0)

**Do competitor mar gaye. Unki ranking abhi baant rahi hai.** Jo abhi ye 3 karega wo utha lega:

```
1. GBP primary category sabse specific chunno       (5 min, ₹0)
2. GBP hours 7 AM – 10 PM karo                       (2 min, ₹0)
3. Har customer se review maango — WhatsApp link     (roz 1 min, ₹0)
```

**Ye 3 kaam ₹20,000 ke domain se 100 guna zyada asar karenge.** Kyunki:
- GBP = local ranking ka **32%**
- Reviews = home services me **36%**
- Domain redirect = **0%** (Google ignore karta hai)

## Option 2 — 🟢 Naya EMD domain kharido (₹800–1,500/saal)

**Ye legal hai, safe hai, aur kaam karta hai** — kyunki ye fresh domain hai, koi purani baggage nahi.

Check karo GoDaddy pe available hai ya nahi:
```
ropatna.in
roservicepatna.in
patnaroservice.in
rorepairpatna.in
```

Top 5 me se 3 competitors ke paas exact-match domain hai. Ye ek asli faayda hai.

**Kaise use karo:**
- Us domain pe **choti si asli site** banao (5-6 page, apna content)
- Ya `rokadoctor.in` pe 301 karo — **par ye fresh domain hai, expired nahi**, isliye safe hai

**Kharch:** ₹800–1,500/saal. Ek domain se zyada mat lena.

## Option 3 — 🟡 Agar sach me competitor ka domain chahiye

**Wo domains kharid hi nahi sakte** (kisi aur ke naam pe registered hain, expire nahi hue).
