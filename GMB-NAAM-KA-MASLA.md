# 🔴 DIKKAT 1 — Naam ka aadha hissa Google ke rules ke against hai

Tera naam:

```
Aqua Perl | Ro Service Centre - Best Ro Service in Patna
└─ asli naam ─┘  └────── ye extra hai, rule todta hai ──────┘
```

## Google ka rule saaf hai

Business name field mein **sirf asli business ka naam** aana chahiye — jo tere signboard, bill, aur website pe likha ho. Service ka naam, sheher ka naam, ya "Best" jaise shabd nahi.

Ye shabd specially flag hote hain:
```
"best"  ·  "top-rated"  ·  "near me"  ·  "24/7"  ·  "cheap"  ·  sheher ka naam
```

**Tere naam mein "Best" bhi hai aur "Patna" bhi.**

## Sach ye hai — abhi ye kaam kar raha hai

Main jhoot nahi bolunga. Do log ne test kiya hai (Joy Hawkins aur Darren Shaw of Whitespark) — **naam mein keyword daalne se ranking sach mein badhti hai.** Keyword hataya to ranking gayab, wapas daala to wapas aa gayi.

Isliye tere competitor bhi ye kar rahe hain. Aur isliye tu abhi shayad dikh bhi raha hoga.

## Par 2026 mein khatra badh gaya hai

Google ne is saal 3 tarah ki saza shuru ki hai:

| Saza | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| **Soft suspension** | Profile dikhta rahega, par tu manage nahi kar payega. Review ka jawab nahi de payega. Google khud keyword hata dega → ranking gir jaayegi |
| **Feature stripping** (2026 mein naya) | "Book Online", messaging, Posts — ye sab band. Profile dikhega par kaam nahi karega |
| **Hard suspension** | Poora listing gayab. **Saare review khatam.** Wapas laane mein hafte lagte hain |

Aur ek jaal hai:

> **Video verification trap** — Google shak kare to live video maangega. Tujhe apna signboard dikhana hoga. **Agar signboard pe "Best Ro Service in Patna" nahi likha hai** (nahi hoga), to verification fail → **listing hamesha ke liye gayab.**

## Sabse bada khatra: competitor report kar sakta hai

Koi bhi teri listing pe **"Suggest an edit"** dabake naam theek karwa sakta hai. Competitor ko pata hai ki naam mein keyword se ranking milti hai — wo report karega.

Tu Patna ke competitive market mein hai. **Ye kabhi bhi ho sakta hai.**
