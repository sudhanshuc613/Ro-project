## ⚠️ Pehle ek imaandaar baat — "dikhega" aur "top pe aayega" alag cheez hai

Ye list batati hai ki **kin shabdon ke liye hamare paas page bana hua hai** — matlab
Google in queries pe hamari site ko *consider* kar sakta hai.

Ye list ye **NAHI** batati ki hum in par **pehle number pe aa jayenge**.

Us farq ko main clear kar deta hoon, kyunki isi pe sab kuch tika hai:

| | Matlab | Kaun control karta hai |
|---|---|---|
| **Page bana hua hai** | Google ko dikhane layak kuch hai | ✅ Maine kar diya |
| **Index ho gaya** | Google ne page apne database me daala | ⏳ GSC + time |
| **Rank kar raha hai** | Top 10 me aa gaya | 🔴 Backlinks + reviews — **tere haath me** |

Abhi hum pehli stage poori kar chuke hain. Doosri chal rahi hai. **Teesri ke liye wo
JustDial/Sulekha/IndiaMART wala 45-minute kaam chahiye — wo main nahi kar sakta.**

Aur ek baat: **mai ye measure nahi kar sakta ki abhi kaun se keyword pe hum kis number pe
hain.** Google bot-scraping block karta hai (maine try kiya — 202 challenge mila). Ye sirf
Search Console me dikhega. Jo koi bina GSC ke ye number batata hai, wo andaza laga raha hai.

## Ye main NAHI keh raha

- ❌ "in 407 keywords pe tu #1 aa jayega"
- ❌ "kal se calls aane lagengi"
- ❌ "SEO ho gaya, ab kuch nahi karna"

**Jo main keh raha hoon:** in 407 phrases ke liye ab hamare paas asli page hai,
asli data ke saath. Pehle 6 phrases pe to kuch tha hi nahi. Ab hai.

Rank hone ke liye ab teen cheez chahiye — aur **teeno tere haath me hain, mere nahi:**

1. **GSC me Request Indexing** — 10 naye area pages (roz 10 ki limit)
2. **5 directory listing** — JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Apple Maps
   (45 min, ₹0, yahi hamara sabse bada gap hai — 0 vs 50 backlinks)
3. **Reviews 50 → 100** — har customer se maango, ek line ka message kaafi hai
