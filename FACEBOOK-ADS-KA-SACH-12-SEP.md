## Tera asli ganit — dono taraf

**Facebook (₹300/din = ₹9,000/mahina):**
```
₹9,000 ÷ ₹8 CPC       = 1,125 click
1,125 × 4% lead rate  = ~45 lead
45 × 15% close        = ~7 job
Cost per job          = ₹1,285
```

**Google Search (₹275/din = ₹8,250/mahina):**
```
₹8,250 ÷ ₹18 CPC      = 458 click
458 × 8% call rate    = ~36 call
36 × 25% close        = ~9 job
Cost per job          = ₹917
```

> **₹200 visit charge pe dono ghata hai.**
> **₹999 package pe dono profit hai.**

# ⚠️ Facebook ki 3 sachai jo koi nahi batata

### 1. RO repair "emergency" service hai — Facebook emergency ke liye kamzor hai

2026 ka data saaf hai:
> *"Google LSA beats Meta 3-5x on cost per booked job for emergency work.
> Meta works when the problem is on a predictable timeline. Meta fails when
> the homeowner is mopping water off the floor."*

Jab kisi ka RO **aaj** kharab hota hai, wo Facebook scroll nahi karta — **Google pe search karta hai.**

**Par** — RO me ek cheez planned hoti hai: **AMC, filter change, yearly servicing.**
Wahan Facebook sach me kaam karta hai. Isiliye Sotset **"RO Service & Washing Package"**
bech raha hai, "emergency repair" nahi.

### 2. Lead 30 minute me thandi ho jaati hai

Facebook lead ne tujhe dhoonda nahi tha — tu uske samne aaya. Agar 5 minute me call
nahi kiya to bhool jayega ki form bhara tha. **30 minute baad zyadatar lead dead.**

Tu akela aadmi hai. Job pe hote hue WhatsApp ka jawab de payega? **Ye imaandari se soch.**
Agar nahi de sakta to Facebook ka paisa barbaad hai.

### 3. Creative chahiye — aur teri asli photo nahi hai

Facebook pe **photo/video hi ad hai.** Text koi nahi padhta.
Teri site pe abhi AI-generated photos hain. Wo Facebook pe bhi chalengi par:
- Log Patna ke local se asli kaam dekhna chahte hain
- Sotset asli photo daal raha hai — wo jeetega

**Facebook chalane se pehle 10-15 asli photo/video chahiye.** Wahi jo GBP ke liye bhi chahiye.
